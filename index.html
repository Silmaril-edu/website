About SILMARIL
Particle Accelerators Through Simulation

SILMARIL is an open educational platform for understanding synchrotron and accelerator physics through interactive simulations and visualizations. Our tools make it easier to explore beam dynamics observables, accelerator components, and particle accelerator infrastructure, connecting learners, researchers, and facilities worldwide.
