Internship Directory

Research Internships and Student Programs

A short list of facilities, institutes, and organizations that offer research internships, summer studentships, or student placements in accelerator science, quantum science, fusion, space, nanoscience, and theoretical physics.

Official links change often, so check each program page for current dates and eligibility.
  1. Geneva, Switzerland
    01
    CERN Summer Studentship

    CERN is the European particle physics laboratory, home to major accelerator facilities and experiments, with summer projects in physics, engineering, computing, and mathematics.

    Visit Program
  2. Hamburg, Germany
    02
    DESY Summer Student Programme

    DESY is a major German research centre for accelerators, photon science, particle physics, astroparticle physics, and scientific computing, with hands-on summer research projects for students.

    Visit Program
  3. Basque Country, Spain
    03
    CIC nanoGUNE Summer Internships

    CIC nanoGUNE is a nanoscience research centre in San Sebastian focused on quantum nanoscience, nanomaterials, nanomedicine, nanooptics, nanodevices, and related nanoscale technologies.

    Visit Program
  4. Okinawa, Japan
    04
    OIST Research Internship

    OIST is an interdisciplinary graduate university with research units across physics, biology, chemistry, neuroscience, mathematics, computation, marine science, and engineering.

    Visit Program
  5. Klosterneuburg, Austria
    05
    ISTA Scientific Internships

    ISTA is a research institute near Vienna for basic science in biology, chemistry, physics, mathematics, computer science, neuroscience, and interdisciplinary fields.

    Visit Program
  6. Munich, Germany
    06
    MCQST Summer Bachelor Program

    MCQST connects Munich research groups working on quantum information, quantum computing, communication, simulation, sensing, quantum matter, and quantum technologies.

    Visit Program
  7. Culham, England
    07
    UKAEA Student Placements

    UKAEA leads UK fusion energy research and hosts student placements in fusion science, engineering, computing, robotics, materials, operations, and supporting technical fields.

    Visit Program
  8. Waterloo, Canada
    08
    Perimeter Undergraduate Summer Programs

    Perimeter Institute is an independent theoretical physics centre focused on fundamental questions in quantum fields, gravity, cosmology, quantum information, and related areas.

    Visit Program
  9. Cadarache, France
    09
    ITER Internships

    ITER is an international fusion project building a large tokamak to demonstrate burning plasma physics, integrating engineering, superconducting magnets, cryogenics, robotics, and plasma science.

    Visit Program
    10. New York, USA
    10
    IBM Internships

    IBM Research and IBM Quantum work on enterprise AI, semiconductors, hybrid cloud, quantum computing, and advanced computing systems, with internships across research and technology teams.

    Visit Program
  10. Tokyo, Japan
    11
    JAXA Internship Program

    JAXA is Japan's space agency, supporting space science, launch vehicles, Earth observation, exploration, aeronautics, and mission operations through short-term student internships.

    Visit Program
